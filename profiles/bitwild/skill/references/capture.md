# What earns a concept

## Source events are not concepts

A daily, planning session, demo, call or conversation earns no concept merely
by occurring. It must produce durable knowledge: a meaningful request, decision,
findings, constraint, term, standing rule, named unknown or reusable guidance.
No durable outcome means no interpreted concept; this is not a gap.
An artifact may still be mirrored when [mirroring.md](mirroring.md) allows it.

A request is ordinarily its own concept. It outlives the conversation that
expressed it and can later be analyzed, specified, implemented, rejected, or
superseded, which is a lifecycle of its own.

## Promote or embed an outcome

Identity decides promotion, not importance. For an outcome such as a
decision, an answer, or an unknown, ask whether it has a lifecycle outside
the concept where it arose.

- Promote it to its own concept when it needs independent status,
  provenance, relationships, reuse, replacement, or history.
- Keep it embedded, as a heading in the concept where it arose, when it has
  no identity of its own and will never be referenced, verified, or
  superseded on its own.
- Start an ambiguous outcome embedded. Un-promoting is free until something
  outside the bundle cites its path.

The same test decides a `Question`. A named unknown with an evidence trail,
several dependents, or an ID cited outside the bundle is normally a concept.
A single unknown that belongs to one concept, with nothing recorded but the
gap, can stay a heading in that concept's body.

## Split a concept

Consider a split when parts of a concept would carry materially different
verification or lifecycle. Frontmatter applies to the whole concept, so
averaging trust across parts loses information. Mixed provenance is fine,
because footnotes keyed to `sources[].id` attribute each claim. Mixed
verification is the signal to split. Size alone is not.

## Interaction Records

An Interaction Record is the narrow case of the same test. Write one only
when an interaction's combined context is itself durable: several linked
outcomes, a negotiation, or a demo whose overall shape matters.

- Never use it as routine minutes.
- When a single outcome mattered, give that outcome its own concept in its
  subject's area and link it straight to its original source. No Interaction
  Record is needed.
- A mirrored transcript preserves an artifact. An Interaction Record
  interprets combined context. One never substitutes for the other.
